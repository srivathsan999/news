GTM Playbook

Story-driven demos that close faster

8 min read · Updated monthly

Modern demos succeed when every touchpoint—from outbound email to onboarding article—shares the same beats. We map each demo chapter to verifiable proof and instrumentation.

1. Anchor your why with a scorecard

Open with a quantifiable shift. Instead of abstract mission statements, reference a scorecard row and explain why it matters. Provide a localized example: “نساعد فرق المبيعات على تقليل وقت الإغلاق بنسبة ٣٥٪ دون إضافة موظفين جدد.”

2. Mirror the product UI

Use the same Tailwind components deployed in-app. This template keeps typography, padding, and iconography consistent, removing surprises when prospects convert.

“After aligning our story arc with the News template, our enterprise win rate improved 19% in two quarters.” — Northwind Cloud

3. Close with an action you can measure

Embed a CTA path that feeds directly into the admin dashboard. In this template, every CTA includes `data-cta` attributes so ops teams can track conversions without extra scripts.

Need the full checklist? Download the component snippets in the `components/` folder and remix.
